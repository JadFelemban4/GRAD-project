## WP3: the scripts

1. **S3b's upper depth edge (P2.1; amendment C1).** C1's bound on the enrichment depth, "Depth <= 1 - (min over yielded calibration Final rows of lambda_cellref - 0.01)", does not imply P2's invariant exactly. The contract's floor is `round(min(0.70, lm - 0.01), 3)` over all rows (`thresholds/contract.py:264`), which can round up by up to 5e-4, and `1.0 - (1.0 - x)` can land one float64 ulp below x. A fit whose depth sits on C1's bound can then build a record that `Engine.__post_init__` refuses, at Run 1. `epa_fit.py` computes the upper edge as `min(C1's bound, 1.0 - lam_floor)`, checked through `engines.Engine` (or `build_candidate`), and reports when that edge is the active one. Since P7 the invariant also requires `lam_floor > 0.0`, so a depth of 1 is refused (P2.4).

3. **E6 row 9's probe of the rich slope on a form A or A+B engine (P3.2; amendment E3, decision 59).** E3 adds `ce_rich_slope` to row 9: a probe under forms A and B, its sigma under C. `CycleCal` refuses a slope other than the shared 0.65 without C, so the probe cannot be a `replace` of the slope alone. It is run as form A+B+C at the engine's own breathing terms (`ve_map` as typed or fitted, 0.0 for A) and the probed slope, as the certificate harness's `HARNESS_SET` sets a form. At 0.65 that retag is the engine's own form to the bit at any finite MAP: C's slope is the same double, and the breathing's B term is the same expression under A+B and A+B+C (`plant.volumetric_efficiency`). `test_fuel_forms.FormsBC` shows the neutral case, A+B and A+B+C at ve_map 0.0 and 0.65 giving form A's doubles over the units grid and over one episode; an A+B engine's own ve_map is not tested that way yet. So `calibrate_epa.py e6` (written in WP3, run in WP4) asserts the retag's identity at 0.65, bit for bit, before it probes: then the probe moves the slope and nothing else. The invariant is never relaxed for it.
