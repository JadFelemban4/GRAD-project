B58 preview study

When is it worth knowing the road ahead? The project's claim is that the answer is set by one ratio, H/τ: how far ahead you can see, over how fast the protected part heats up. This page shows what the simulation says on the locked scenario, and every place the simulator can be laid beside the car's own logs.

∅negative

What this page is not

Not Phase D's answer

The ten agents trained at 110 km/h, where the constraint never binds, and at a 0.2 s step while the protocol scores at 1.0 s. They are out of distribution twice over. The retrain at 130 km/h and dt = 1.0 is what settles it.

Hand-written rows are one rollout

They do not read the preference weights, so their zero spread is structural. It is not a sign of stability.

Turbine temperature is modelled

No sensor on this car reads it. Its heat capacity, c_turb = 6000 J/K, is assumed, and it sets τ, the denominator of the claim.

Three calibrations need attention

The spark map needs a refit on the modelled charge temperature, the enrichment table needs regenerating on real timestamps, and the oil node needs more thermal mass. None of these has been changed.
