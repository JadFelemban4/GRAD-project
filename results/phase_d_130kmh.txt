READ THIS BEFORE QUOTING ANY ROW ABOVE.

1. THESE AGENTS ARE OUT OF DISTRIBUTION TWICE OVER, AND NEITHER IS SMALL.

   They trained at 110 km/h, where the baseline peaks at 840 C against an
   850 C trigger and spends 0.0 % of the episode above it. They had nothing
   to protect against. They are scored here at 130 km/h, where the baseline
   reaches 884 C and spends 66.3 % of the episode above the trigger.

   And train.py never passes `dt`, so all ten trained at dt = 0.2 s while this
   protocol runs dt = 1.0 s. Since AUDIT.md M16 made the slew limit
   `SLEW * dt`, the same network output moves every actuator FIVE TIMES
   FURTHER PER STEP here than it could during training. The smoothness penalty
   is scaled by dt as well. This is the dt inconsistency the open-questions
   list already flags, and it lands directly on the ablation.

   SO THIS IS NOT PHASE D'S ANSWER. It is what the existing runs are worth.

2. WHAT IT NEVERTHELESS SETTLES, and it is the stronger half.

   Every trained agent beats every hand-written policy by a wide margin --
   44.9 to 70.5 % against current-grade's 34.0 %. AUDIT.md C3 argued that
   hand-written policies cannot settle the preview question, and on the
   hand-written table preview LOSES to current-grade by 0.4 points. Trained,
   the gap to current-grade is +25.1 points. The hand-written comparison was
   not merely imprecise, it had the wrong sign.

3. WHAT IT DOES NOT SETTLE, AND THE DIRECTION IS WORTH NOTING.

   Paired by seed, sighted minus blinded is -0.2, +0.7, -0.3, -5.3, +0.5.
   Four of five seeds fall within +-0.7 points. The mean is -0.9 with a 95 %
   interval of -4.0 to +2.2, and both the t-test and the signed-rank test are
   far from significance at n = 5.

   PREVIEW IS INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM ZERO HERE, and the point estimate is
   NEGATIVE. In the damage-versus-fuel plane the sighted agent of seeds 2 and
   4 reaches the same damage cut as its blinded twin while burning MORE fuel.

   That is a result about H/tau and should be reported as one if it survives
   the retrain -- not a failure.

4. THE HAND-WRITTEN ROWS HAVE ZERO IQR BY CONSTRUCTION. They do not read the
   preference vector, so all twenty episodes are one rollout repeated. Only
   the agents, which carry w in the observation, vary across the twenty. Do
   not read their zero spread as stability.

5. TWO AGENTS BEAT THE BASELINE ON BOTH AXES. Seeds 0 and 1, sighted and
   blinded alike, cut damage by about 45 % while burning 1.5 to 2.5 % LESS
   fuel than the baseline. Every other protecting policy in the table, hand-
