The B58 preview study, on this branch

When is it worth knowing the road ahead? The project's claim is that one ratio settles it, H/τ: how far ahead you can see, over how fast the protected part heats up. This page shows what this branch's three preregistered experiments found, and every place the simulator can be laid beside the car's own logs.

Part 1 · the agents

Three preregistered ablations of the preview channel

Each experiment trained eight pairs of agents. In a pair both agents come from the same seed and code; one sees the road ahead, one does not. The rules of each test were committed before its agents trained, and 50 damage units were set in advance as the smallest effect worth caring about.

Drives that would settle what the logs cannot

  1. More independent readings at high air flow: the top of the compressor envelope rests on a handful of readings per bin (the ceiling chart shows how many).
  2. The knock question: a drive logging both ignition-angle channels fast enough to see a retard event, which the current logs sample too slowly.
  3. Steady operating points above 75 kPa, where every model figure is now extrapolation.
  4. The empty compressor bin above the air-mass sensor's ceiling; probably unreachable with this sensor.

new_drive.py scores a candidate drive against these before it is added.

Part 4 · caveats

What this page is not

  • Not a verdict that preview does not help. The sentences that may be said are the preregistered readings quoted under each pair chart.
  • Not measured turbine temperatures. The car has no sensor on the turbine housing, and the housing's heat capacity in the model is an assumed number.
  • Not agents scored in the step they learned in: every agent trained at a 0.2 s step and was scored at 1.0 s, a known and unresolved limit shared by both arms (AUDIT2.md H2-2).
  • Not a replication: C4's agents are Phase D2's agents trained longer, and they had not converged by the preregistered rule.
  • Not a test of the breathing model at part load: both pressure channels on this car sit before the throttle.
  • Not a road anyone drives: the locked scenario stands for sustained heavy load, and it is hotter than every recorded drive on purpose.
