The simulator against the Supra's logs

The engine simulator set beside the logged drives of the team's test car, a Toyota GR Supra. Five comparisons, one chart each, ordered from the strongest kind of evidence to the weakest.

82,984 logged samples · 7 drives · 26 steady points · gearbox ZF 8HP51 · engine B58, 3.0 L

  1. independent checkThe gearbox: the ratios the car ran, against the eight Toyota publishes86.7 % of moving samples within ±4% of a published ratio

  3. fitBoost against air flow: the simulator's ceiling on the car's envelopeRMS 0.135 in pressure ratio; the top bins rest on a handful of readings each
  4. fitEnrichment: the mixture under boost, against the model's maplargest cell gap 0.076 in λ

  • independent checkthe model side came from a published source, or from a model written without this data; the logs never touched it
  • fitthe model side was fitted to this car's logs; the chart shows the fit and tests nothing
  • consistency checkthe comparison cancels the model it appears to test (CLAUDE.md mistake 12)

Not charted, and why

  • Turbine housing temperature, the quantity the project protects: the car has no sensor for it.
  • Knock: the model's knock integral and the car's own retard are unrelated (AUDIT.md H5). A negative result, and no script charts it yet.
  • Oil temperature over a whole drive, modelled against measured: not computed yet.
  • Air mass flow and coolant: equal by construction, so a chart of them would prove nothing.
