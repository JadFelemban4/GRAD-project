الخطة القديمة كُتبت وهي تصدّق أربعة أشياء لم تعد صحيحة. المساهمة نفسها — معيار H/τ — باقية كما هي، والبوابة التي نعبر منها إلى مشروع ناجح لا تزال Phase D. ما تغيّر هو ماذا يثبت ماذا، وأيّ عمل يسبق أيّ عمل.

سجلّ تاريخي — اقرأ هذا أولًا. هذه الخطة كُتبت في 14 سبتمبر، قبل Phase D. نُفِّذت Phase D في 21–22 سبتمبر ونتيجتها صفرية: مع وكلاء مدرَّبين بميزانية C1 ‏(50 000 خطوة)، لا ينفصل الـpreview عن ضجيج البذور — 5 من 8 بذور موجبة، اختبار الإشارة p = 0.3633، والتبديل p = 0.4922. والذراع المُعمّاة لم تكن معمّاة فعلًا: الطريق نفسه في كل حلقة، وحالته الحرارية ساعة، فكان بوسع الوكيل أن يحفظ موعد التلّ (results/PREREGISTRATION.md، الحدّ 7). خطوات هذه الصفحة وتقديرات أزمنتها سجلّ لما كان يُعتقد يومها، لا تعليمات. تنبيه: بعض الأرقام داخل الخطوات والأسباب — بوابة المكافأة، وعدد الاختبارات، والعكس تحت الشحن — حُدِّثت إلى قيمها الحالية في 22 سبتمبر، أمّا تقديرات الأزمنة فلم تُحدَّث وتبقى تاريخًا. الأرقام الحالية في CLAUDE.md وFULL_RUN.txt، وجدول الأرقام أدناه حُدِّث في 22 سبتمبر.

الرصيد الذي سمّيناه «مصادقة» لا يرى النموذج الذي يُفترض أن يصادقه

map_from_airflow() يعكس نفس العلاقة التي يستخدمها run_cycle()، فتُختصر الكفاءة الحجمية ونسبة الغاز المتبقّي ودرجة حرارة الشحنة من المعادلة كلها. أُجبرت eta_v على 0.50 ثم 1.20 فلم يتحرّك الرصيد عن 1.374 %. احذف نموذج التنفّس بالكامل: الرقم نفسه. الرصيد فحص اتساق بين قناتين في ECU — وهذا يكسب ثلاثة أشياء حقيقية، لكن «المحاكي يطابق السيارة» ليس واحدًا منها.

13

قناة حرارة الشحنة كانت مخرج الضاغط

Intake air temperature before throttle valve تقرأ 149 °C تحت الشحن. مبرّد B58 داخل مجمّع السحب، بعد الخانق، فـ«قبل الخانق» يعني قبل المبرّد. التصحيح نقل نطاق نقاط التشغيل إلى 30–75 kPa وبوابة الإثراء إلى 180 kPa، وقرّب العكس تحت الشحن من +23.7 % إلى +1.9 % مقابل قناة الـboost في السيارة نفسها. نموذج التنفّس بُرِّئ، لم يُدَن.

التدريب — المعطِّل الوحيد

ليلتان · 46 ساعة معالج
  • ثبّت stable-baselines3 وأزل التعليق عن سطريه في requirements.txt.
  • الليلة: python train.py --steps 50000 --seed 0. النتيجة ستكون سيئة، وهذا هو المطلوب.
  • ثم عشر تشغيلات: خمس بذور × (مع preview / بدونه). بذرة لكل شخص، يُتّفق عليها قبل أن يبدأ أحد.
  • شغّل test_reward.py قبل تصديق أيّ منحنى: أربع من أربع، والمحايد −0.00038.

البوابة: عشرة مجلدات تشغيل، كل واحد فيه checkpoint ومنحنى يرتفع لا يستوي. الوكلاء ليسوا مطالَبين بأن يكونوا جيدين بعد.

إن لم يتقارب: اخفض --lr 1e-4 أولًا، ثم قصّر الحلقة. لا تعدّل المكافأة — هكذا عاد الاختراق في المرة الثانية.

03

اكنس H نفسه، لا τ فقط جديد

نصف يوم كود · ساعة تشغيل
  • حاليًا PREVIEW_S = (2, 5, 15, 30) ثابتة، والسياسات تقرأ الخانتين الأخيرتين. اجعلها معاملًا.
  • اكنس H عند τ ثابت: 2 / 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 s، بنفس السياسات الثلاث المكتوبة يدويًا.
  • ارسم المجموعتين معًا: نقاط «τ متغيّر، H ثابت» ونقاط «H متغيّر، τ ثابت» على نفس محور H/τ.

البوابة: جدول H عند c_turb = 6000 وآخر عند 800، ورأي مكتوب في سؤال واحد: هل ينطبق الطريقان؟

لماذا يستحق: الاعتراض الأقوى على الشكل الحالي أنك حرّكت معاملًا في نموذجك، لا المعلومة. H هو المعلومة. وإن انطبق الطريقان فأنت تملك اختبار انهيار على نبتة واحدة — تأمين كامل على Phase F قبل كتابة سطر من battery.py.

05

قرِّر ما هو التطبيق الحي، وجمّده جديد

اجتماع واحد
  • الوضع الحالي: قراءة فقط، لا يخزّن بيانات خام، ثلاثة أنواع إنذار مفصولة بجمهورها، 49/49 اختبار تمرّ.
  • التوصية: يبقى عرضًا للمناقشة + فصلًا قصيرًا يشرح المقدِّر الافتراضي — وتُجمَّد ميزاته اليوم.
  • يُكتب في وثيقة أنه لا يدّعي ثقة أكثر من المحاكي: 8 من 11، وثلاثة إخفاقات موثّقة، ورصيد مقيس عند 30–75 kPa فقط.

البوابة: سطر واحد مكتوب يقول ما هو التطبيق وما ليس هو، وقائمة ميزات مغلقة.

الخطر الحقيقي: ليس أن يفشل، بل أن ينجح ويأكل الأسابيع التي تخصّ Phase D. هو رفع للسقف، والأرضية لم تُصبَّ بعد.

بعد بوابة D فقط

ما يُؤجَّل عمدًا

البطارية — بمواصفة أرخص

نفس عقد predict(). أسقط زوج RC إن قاومك، واحتفظ بحدّ Arrhenius — هو الجزء الحامل. مهمّتها الآن التعميم لا المدى، فنقطة أو نقطتان على المنحنى تكفيان لاختبار الانطباق.

جدول Phase D

ثلاثة خطوط أساس، عشرون حلقة مجمّدة، وسيط ومدى ربيعي على خمس بذور، والـablation جنبًا إلى جنب. صفّ واحد وفقرة تحته. هذه هي الأرضية.

الكتابة

فصل الحدود يُكتب قبل فصل النتائج. مكتوبًا أخيرًا يقرأ كاعتذار، ومكتوبًا أولًا يقرأ كسيطرة على المادة.

الحجّة التي تغيّرت

ماذا يصل المحرك فعلًا على محور H/τ

السبب الذي بيع به قرار «نبتتين» كان أن المحرك محبوس تحت 0.6. الكنس الموجود في generality_test.py يقول غير ذلك.

النقاط الممتلئة هي مواضع كنس c_turb على المحور (H/τ = 0.60 و1.43 و4.47). نسخة 14 سبتمبر كتبت فوقها أفضلية الـpreview من جدول H2 الثابت عند 1123 K، وذلك الجدول باطل (AUDIT.md ‏C1 وM12 وH2-5) فحُذفت أرقامه؛ شغّل generality_test.py واقرأ ما يطبعه. النقطتان المجوّفتان على اليسار (0.20 و0.06) لم تنتجا رقمًا في كنس 14 سبتمبر الباطل نفسه، لأن الحرارة لم تبلغ الحدّ هناك على السيناريو القديم. هذا تاريخ لا نتيجة: على سيناريو اليوم (130 كم/س) لم يُعَد قياس هاتين النقطتين، فلا يُقال إن القيد لا يُلزم عندهما — أعد تشغيل generality_test.py قبل تكرار ذلك. مدى البطارية المرسوم مأخوذ من مواصفة خارطة الطريق نفسها (τ ≈ 9–38 دقيقة، وأفق من 30 ثانية إلى 60 دقيقة).

engine under supervision · 14 september 2026

The destination has not moved. The order has.

The old plan was written believing four things that are no longer true. The contribution itself — the H/τ criterion — is untouched, and the gate that makes this a passing project is still Phase D. What changed is which evidence proves what, and which work has to come before which.

Historical record — read this first. This plan was written on 14 September, before Phase D. Phase D ran on 21–22 September and returned a null: with agents trained to the C1 budget (50 000 steps), preview does not separate from seed noise — 5 of 8 seeds positive, sign test p = 0.3633, permutation p = 0.4922. And the blinded arm was not truly blind: the road was the same in every episode and its thermal state works as a clock, so the agent could memorise when the hill comes (results/PREREGISTRATION.md, limit 7). The steps and time estimates on this page record what was believed then; they are not instructions. Note: some in-line figures in the steps and reasons — the reward gate, the test count, the boosted inversion — were updated to current values on 22 September; the time estimates were not, and remain history. why

Five things moved under the plan

Each one is recorded in the repository with evidence, not opinion. Every figure here is printed by a script.

12

The residual we called validation cannot see the model it was said to validate

map_from_airflow() inverts the same relation run_cycle() uses, so volumetric efficiency, residual fraction and charge temperature all cancel out of the comparison. Forcing eta_v to 0.50, then to 1.20, leaves the residual at 1.374 %. Delete the breathing model entirely and the number does not move. It is a consistency check between two ECU channels — which earns three real things, but "the simulator matches the car" is not one of them.

13

The charge-temperature channel was a compressor outlet

Intake air temperature before throttle valve reads 149 °C under boost. The B58 carries its cooler inside the intake manifold, downstream of the throttle, so "before throttle" is before the cooler. Correcting it moved the operating-point span to 30–75 kPa and the enrichment gate to 180 kPa, and brought the boosted inversion from +23.7 % to +1.9 % against the car's own boost channel. The breathing model was cleared, not convicted.

14/9

Seven of the eleven "published" bands have no source

The citation pass opened the sources row by row: two confirmed to a page, one partial, one measured from our own logs, and seven with no source at all — including row 4, the knock-limited spark, which a deliberate search failed to support. The old plan treats "8 of 11" as banked evidence. In the thesis, an unsourced row is an engineering-judgement band, and it says so.

app

A complete live application appeared, and it is in no planning document

app/ runs the same physics alongside the car in real time and estimates what the car cannot report: turbine temperature, knock margin, accumulated damage. Read-only, no raw data written to disk, 49/49 tests passing. It is the strongest demonstration available in a BSc defence — and the fastest way to eat Phase D if it is left to grow. It needs a written decision, not neglect.

τ

The number that sold the two-plant decision is void

The roadmap justifies the battery by a sweep that moved the preview advantage from 7.6 to 49.9 points — both measured on the wrong four-cylinder engine. The table that replaced them at the 1123 K limit — the H2 table from generality_test.py — is void too (AUDIT.md C1, M12, H2-5). There is no live figure here yet: re-run the script, read what it prints, and rewrite the argument only from a live run.

the new plan

Five tracks, in this order

Only track 1 is sequential, because it waits on machines. The other four run alongside it — and that is the whole gain from re-cutting the plan.

01

Training — the only blocker

2 nights · 46 CPU-hours
  • Install stable-baselines3 and uncomment its two lines in requirements.txt.
  • Tonight: python train.py --steps 50000 --seed 0. The result will be poor, and that is the point.
  • Then ten runs: five seeds × (preview / no-preview). One seed per person, agreed before anyone starts.
  • Run test_reward.py before believing any curve: four of four, neutral at −0.00038.

Gate: ten run directories, each with a checkpoint and a learning curve that rises rather than going sideways. The agents do not have to be good yet.

If it will not converge: lower --lr 1e-4 first, then shorten the episode. Do not edit the reward — that is how the hack came back the second time.

02

Freeze the twenty episodes now re-ordered

30 minutes
  • Twenty episodes chosen once: seeds, grades, ambient temperatures, preference weights.
  • Written into a file, committed with a message saying they are frozen, then treated as read-only.
  • Done while track 1's runs are going overnight — not after them.

Gate: the twenty are in version control before anyone has seen a result.

Why it moved earlier: changing the test set after seeing results is the one mistake this project cannot recover from. Choosing now deletes the temptation instead of requiring resistance to it — and an examiner will ask when it was chosen.

03

Sweep H itself, not only τ new

half a day of code · one hour of runs
  • Today PREVIEW_S = (2, 5, 15, 30) is fixed and the policies read the last two entries. Make it a parameter.
  • Sweep H at fixed τ: 2 / 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 s, using the same three hand-written policies.
  • Plot both sets together: "τ varied, H fixed" points and "H varied, τ fixed" points on the same H/τ axis.

Gate: an H table at c_turb = 6000 and another at 800, plus a written answer to one question: do the two routes overlap?

Why it is worth it: the strongest objection to the current figure is that you varied a parameter of your own model, not the information. H is the information. And if the two routes overlap you hold a collapse test on a single plant — full insurance on Phase F before a line of battery.py is written.

04

Repair the evidence

one day, spread out
  • Two minutes settles the compression ratio: read the rated output off the car's plate. 285 kW means 10.2:1; 250 kW means 11.0:1 — and then the knock model is running the wrong ratio.
  • Row 4 (knock-limited spark): drop it from the validation table or relabel it an internal consistency check.
  • Write it plainly in the limitations: there is no part-load test of the breathing model, because both logged pressure channels sit before the throttle.
  • Adopt 1123 K as the threshold everywhere, and quote it with every preview figure.

Gate: every row of validation_table.md carries a source column pointing at REFERENCES.md, and every unsourced row is described as engineering judgement.

Working note: when a figure changes, grep the tree for the old value yourself and read every hit. A green verify_docs.py proves only that the patterns which exist found nothing — five lines escaped that way on 14 September.

05

Decide what the live app is, and freeze it new

one meeting
  • Where it stands: read-only, no raw data stored, three alert types separated by audience, 49/49 tests passing.
  • Recommendation: keep it as the defence demonstration plus a short chapter on the virtual sensor — and freeze its feature set today.
  • Write down that it claims no more confidence than the simulator: 8 of 11, three documented misses, and a residual measured only at 30–75 kPa.

Gate: one written line saying what the app is and what it is not, and a closed feature list.

The real risk: not that it fails, but that it succeeds and eats the weeks that belong to Phase D. It raises the ceiling; the floor has not been poured yet.

What is deliberately deferred

The battery — at a cheaper spec

The same predict() contract. Drop the RC pair if it fights you and keep the Arrhenius term — that is the load-bearing part. Its job is generality now, not range, so one or two points on the curve are enough to test the collapse.

The Phase D table

Three baselines, twenty frozen episodes, median and interquartile range over five seeds, and the ablation side by side. One table, one paragraph under it. That is the floor.

The writing

The limitations chapter is written before the results chapter. Written last it reads as an apology; written first it reads as control of the material.

the argument that changed

What the engine actually reaches on the H/τ axis

The two-plant decision was sold on the engine being stuck below 0.6. The sweep already sitting in generality_test.py says otherwise.

The filled dots mark where the c_turb sweep sits on the axis (H/τ = 0.60, 1.43 and 4.47). The 14 September version printed the preview edge above them, from the fixed-limit H2 table at 1123 K; that table is void (AUDIT.md C1, M12, H2-5), so its figures were removed — run generality_test.py and read what it prints. On that same 14 September sweep, now void, the two hollow dots on the left (0.20 and 0.06) produced no figure, because the temperature never reached the limit on the old scenario. That is history, not a result: those points have not been re-measured on today's 130 km/h scenario, so do not claim the constraint fails to bind there — re-run generality_test.py before repeating it. The battery band is drawn from the roadmap's own spec (τ ≈ 9–38 min, horizons from 30 s to 60 min).

What this means for the battery

It is not cancelled — but it is no longer "the instrument that makes the criterion testable". It is the second plant that tests whether the curve holds across different physics. That allows a cheaper spec and a later slot.

What it means for the expected objection

"You varied a parameter of your model, not the information." The answer is not words: sweep H as well and show that both routes give the same curve. That is track 3.

What it means for the fallback

The old fallback was "a weaker generality claim". The new one is stronger: a criterion demonstrated on one plant through two independent routes along the axis — sturdier than a second plant built in a hurry.

The cut order, re-ordered

Decided now and in cold blood, so a bad month becomes a smaller thesis rather than a panic.

1stPareto front and preference conditioningLose the trade-off figure. RQ1, RQ2 and RQ3 all survive intact. Unchanged from the old plan.
2ndThe MPC oracleLose the empirical answer to "is learning necessary". Answer it analytically and flag it as a limitation.
3rdSurrogate ensembles → train on the plant directlyLose RQ3. Slower training, no loss of validity.
4thThe battery — moved up the cut listIt was 5th; it is now 4th. The reason: sweeping H (track 3) now carries the generality argument the battery used to carry alone. What survives: a criterion on one plant through two independent routes.
5thThe learned agent — moved downIt was 4th. It is no longer cut before the battery, because it is half of gate D, and gate D is the floor. The hand-policy result stays as the insurance.
neverThe vehicle campaign, and the no-writing-to-the-ECU ruleThe first is what makes this a real-vehicle project; the second is a condition of its existence.
